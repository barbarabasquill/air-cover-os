# Air Cover section guide

Each section has a job, the inputs it takes, what Claude writes, and a limit.
Drop any section with no real content.

## Header
- **Shows:** Air Cover, the subtitle "Weekly intel from marketing to help you win.",
  the week, and a numbered contents line.
- **Limit:** one line of contents.

## 1. TL;DR
- **Job:** the whole email in three lines, for reps who read nothing else.
- **Inputs:** none. Written last, from the sections below.
- **Format:** what moved / what's hot / what to do this week.

## 2. Pipeline Pulse
- **Job:** show marketing's contribution and where it's trending.
- **Inputs:** marketing-sourced pipeline, target, influenced pipeline,
  MQLs and SQLs this week and last week, optional note.
- **Claude writes:** a one-row stat strip, week-over-week change, and two
  sentences of "so what". Percent to target = sourced ÷ target.
- **Limit:** four numbers, two sentences.

## 3. Accounts to Work This Week
- **Job:** the most valuable section. Tell reps who to call and why now.
- **Inputs per account:** name, signal, owner or territory, optional detail.
- **Signals:** intent surge, pricing-page visit, webinar attendee, content
  download, funding, new executive, hiring for the pain, competitor eval.
- **Claude writes:** a one-line suggested opener per account, tied to the signal.
- **Limit:** ten accounts. Group by owner or territory when there are more than five.

## 4. Speed-to-Lead
- **Job:** keep inbound from going cold, without shaming anyone.
- **Inputs:** average first response time, % of MQLs untouched after 48 hours,
  goal for next week.
- **Claude writes:** one sentence on why it matters and one encouraging nudge.
- **Limit:** three numbers, two sentences.

## 5. What's Launching
- **Job:** no rep is surprised by a campaign their prospect saw first.
- **Inputs per item:** name, type, launch date, audience, asset link.
- **Claude writes:** a one-line talk track per item.
- **Limit:** five items.

## 6. Play of the Week
- **Job:** one best practice reps can use today.
- **Inputs:** play type (email, call opener, LinkedIn, follow-up, voicemail),
  topic, optional campaign it ties to.
- **Claude writes:** when to use it, plus ready-to-copy text under 90 words.

## 7. Spotlight (one per week, rotate)
- **Win Story:** deal, segment, what marketing touched, what worked.
- **Competitive Quick Take:** competitor, the move, how to respond.
  Firecrawl may confirm the move; link the source.
- **Voice of the Customer:** top objection or theme and the best answer.
  Gong may supply themes; never name the customer without approval.
- **Content Spotlight:** the asset, why it works, when to send it.
- **Limit:** under 120 words.

## 8. Wrap Up
- **Job:** close the loop and start a conversation.
- **Inputs:** optional one-sentence story of the week; closing question.
- **Claude writes:** three or four sentences on the week, then the question,
  then "I read every reply."
- **Rotating questions:**
  - What's the one thing marketing could do next week to help you close faster?
  - What objection did you hear most this week?
  - Which account do you wish you had more intel on?
  - What content do you wish existed?
  - Which campaign is showing up in your conversations?

## Footer
- Sender name and role, asset library link, request-a-campaign link.
- "Data as of [date]. Sources: [list]."
